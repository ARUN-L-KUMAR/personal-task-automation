FAQ

Common questions

Is my Google data private and secure?
Yes. G-ONE uses Google's official OAuth 2.0 — we never see or store your password. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We are SOC 2 Type II certified and never sell your data to third parties.
Does it work with non-Google tools?
Currently G-ONE is optimised for Google Workspace. Support for Microsoft 365, Notion, and Slack is on the roadmap for Q3 2026. Sign up to get early access.
Can I cancel or downgrade anytime?
Absolutely. Cancel or downgrade anytime from your account settings. No questions asked, no penalties. If you cancel a paid plan, you'll retain access until the end of your billing period.
What makes this different from Zapier or Make?
Zapier and Make require you to build automations yourself. G-ONE uses AI to understand your work patterns and acts proactively without you setting up any rules. It's the difference between a workflow tool and an AI colleague.
Is there a free trial for the Pro plan?
Yes — every new account gets a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan with no credit card required. After the trial you'll be automatically moved to the Free plan unless you choose to upgrade.
